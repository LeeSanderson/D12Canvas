# Moving and reconnecting connectors

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 11, 12
Map: [Better standard components](../map.md)

## Question

How does a user move a connector and reconnect its ends to other shapes?

Decide:

- endpoint handles: when they show, their size, and dragging one onto another shape or port
- dragging the connector body: does it detach both ends and move freely, move only floating ends, or do nothing
- reconnecting several selected connectors at once, or one at a time only
- undo granularity: one command per reconnect
- what happens with a locked connector or a locked target shape
